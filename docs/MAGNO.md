# The MAGNO bible

Generated by `npm run docs` from the emotion table in `src/game/magno-table.ts`, the rig it names and the tuning it quotes. The suite re-renders this file and compares it byte for byte, holds every face to the rig's own vocabulary and every word to the recipes' own, and fails the build on a face or a word the code cannot back (R-CH-04).

One eye and a horseshoe magnet — a perfect minimal mascot in the ingredients. The table below is his whole language: twelve words, twelve faces, each spoken only at the moment that earns it. The settled face is not in the table because silence is its word: calm, not chatty.

## The laws

Quoted from the code that enforces them:

- **The voice** — every word is a synthesized chirp of at most a quarter second, band-limited 1000–8000 Hz, riding a bus at -24 dB — 6 under the music bed by law, and silent while a stinger owns the moment. The same word twice inside two seconds is machine-gun fire, not speech (R-AU-06). No human speech, ever, anywhere: the language is wordless, absolutely.
- **The eye** — the pupil dilates up to 135% of rest at full charge, follows the line of flight, squeezes shut for 220 ms on a landing that hurt, swaps to the comic X after a fall deeper than 9 m, and carries the clear gleam for 2.4 s (R-CH-03). The X is a curve, never a wound: the daze is comedy, not gore.
- **The lag** — the head trails the body by 30 ms and the hands by 80 ms, while the eye leads both; settled, the head counter-breathes with the body (R-CH-05, PRD 7.4).
- **The idle performance** — a breath a little over two seconds, a blink inside 4–7 s of settling (some blinks carrying a glance at the nearest steel), and one character beat every 20 ± 4 s: a look-around, plus a flip in place at full motion (R-CH-02).
- **The plush test** — the silhouette reads at 64 px: horseshoe head, capsule body, the red north and blue south the only such pair on screen. If it could not be merchandised, the rig is wrong, not the resolution (R-CH-01).
- **The physics never lies** — every face, blink, breath and flip is rig layer. The simulation capsule moves exactly as the physics says it does, on every tier, in every biome (R-CH-10).
