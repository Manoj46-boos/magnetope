# The economy

Generated by `npm run docs` from the Scrap formula, the cosmetics table and the progression bot’s own replay (`src/game/economy-replay.ts`). The suite re-renders this file and compares it byte for byte, and the economy sim replays the schedule the same way the doc tells it (R-EC-07).

One currency, one law: Scrap only ever buys cosmetics, so it never gates content. Biomes open on stars alone — the gates are 0 / 15 / 34 / 57 / 80 of the 210 on offer.

## The schedule

| Fixed | Per-clear Scrap | a first clear pays 23–49 by stars, a replay 4–8; playing without the snap assist earns the 5% bonus |
| Fixed | Star stamps | three a level, on the clear card, on the score’s own tonic |
| Variable | Fall grades | the comedy verdict, C to SSS, on the card and in the locker’s requirement paths |
| Variable | Reward looks | 8 earned by playing: stars, clears, perfects, a level’s first clear, the best fall grade |
| Variable | The highlight strip | the session’s funniest falls, best first, in memory only |
| Scheduled | Seasonal slots | 4 looks at their season’s price, in season only |

## The progression bot’s verdict

The bot clears every level once under a star policy, then replays, buying the shelf in price order — no grade is assumed, so grade-gated looks sit outside its count:

| Policy | First pass pays | Shelf | Replay pays | Rounds to finish the shelf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3★, assist on | 3430 | 2900 | 8 | 0 |
| 2★, assist on | 2520 | 2900 | 6 | 1 |
| 1★, assist on | 1610 | 2900 | 4 | 5 |

The three-star first pass covers the shelf on its own; the two-star pass needs a couple of replay rounds; the one-star pass is the honest floor. Cosmetic prices sit on that curve on purpose: the shelf is a collector’s run, never a gate.
