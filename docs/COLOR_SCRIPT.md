## The beat treatments

How each biome dresses the climb’s clockwork — establish, twist, escalate, resolve — in colour the level ledger already measures:

| Biome | Establish | Twist | Escalate | Resolve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| scrapyard | burnt-orange dusk states the yard before the first flip | cold blue steel surfaces as the climb’s own material | the sun lower, the fog thicker, the reds deeper | the crane silhouette holds the last warmth |
| suburbia | morning lawn: saturated green under a high sun | terracotta roofs break the green as the yards climb | the greens deepen toward the city edge | the water tower stands against the bright horizon |
| metropolis | committed blue-violet night; the neon warm | signs and lit windows carry the only saturated accent | wet-asphalt reflections double the neon upward | the dish silhouette against the pre-dawn |
| playground | warm grey asphalt in full daylight; candy at a distance | the magenta and cyan rides enter one by one | the full triangle at saturation, earned by the climb | the big wheel silhouette in golden hour |
| stratosphere | near-monochrome dawn blue; the world quieted | sunrise gold appears at under 3% of the frame | the aurora curtains follow the climb skyward | the narrowest gamut: MAGNO’s poles are the brightest things left |

## The laws

- **The poles** — MAGNO’s red north (#e53935) and blue south (#1e88e5) are the only red-and-blue pair on screen, whatever the biome: magnetism is the game’s identity and it keeps its colours.
- **The effect palette** — attract reads cyan, repel amber; bright skies take deeper colours with normal blending so the lines never wash out.
- **The store card’s own grade** — contrast 1.24, saturation 1.28, bloom 0.52: stronger than any biome because the tile is shrunk to a phone row.
- **Contrast between biomes is total** — the Scrapyard’s burnt orange against Suburbia’s lawn green is a biome shift the player feels in their bones; within a biome the gamut is narrow on purpose.
