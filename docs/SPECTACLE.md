## The spacing law

No two signature beats inside 20 seconds of each other (R-SM-06). The scripted beats — the biome establishing shot, the one-time first catch, the ending film — are authored, so the ledger records them rather than gating them. The dynamic beat, the big-fall slow motion, is the one that yields: a fall straight after a staged moment stays at full speed, because two spectacles back to back read as noise. The route linter replays all seventy recorded routes against the delivered timeline and pins the measured reality — tall levels swing more than twelve metres between catches by design, so the bar allows at most two suppressed slow-mos per route while the ledger delivers the twenty-second rule at runtime.
